Entry for [The Moon Finally Answers Back](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes one original single-line comeback, in the Moon's own voice, to `/output/entry.txt`.

> I've been your neighbor for four billion years. Stop howling your 3 a.m. promises at me — I heard the wish, and the answer is still no.

135 characters, one line. The Moon here is the sarcastic old neighbour upstairs: four billion years of listening to wishes, howls, and 3 a.m. promises, and his reply is the neighbour's classic — I heard you, the answer is still no. The turn lands in the last six words, dry and done with it, exactly how a Moon that has heard everything would sound. Nothing is explained; the refusal is the punchline.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
